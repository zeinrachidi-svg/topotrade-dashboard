NVIDIA (NVDA) — essentially flat, closing at $218.29 (‑0.03%), after trading between $218.05 and $222.00. No fresh catalyst that session; the recent notable news remains the September 3 announcement of NVIDIA's $12.9 billion acquisition of Hugging Face.

Moderna (MRNA) — the session's big mover, up roughly 8–8.6% to about $147.37, after Argus reiterated a Buy rating with a $180 target following FDA approval of Moderna's updated 2026–2027 COVID-19 vaccine, its fifth commercial mRNA product. ASML Holding (ASML/ASME) — closed at €1,474.20, in a €1,463.00–€1,497.20 range. A new partnership with TSMC on 12-inch High-NA EUV photomask technology supports long-term demand expectations, and 2026 sales and margin guidance has been raised on AI-driven chip demand, though shares trade at a rich ~53x earnings versus semiconductor peers.

Broader market — the S&P 500 climbed 0.86% to 7,656.98 and the Nasdaq rose 0.96% to 26,333.04, snapping a four-day losing streak. The main driver was hotter-than-expected August inflation data (PPI +0.4%, annual rate up to 5.4%; CPI +0.4%), which paradoxically lifted equities even as it reduced the odds of a Fed rate cut at the September 15–16 FOMC meeting. Chip and biotech names were among the session's standouts.
