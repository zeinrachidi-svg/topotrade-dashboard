Portfolio

SpaceX (SPCX) closed Friday's session around $150.15, essentially flat to slightly up from Thursday's $148.18 close, trading between $145.92 and $151.85 intraday. Analyst sentiment stays lopsidedly bullish — 29 buy ratings against 2 sells — with no single headline catalyst that day.

Intel (INTC) gained on the session, opening up about 3.3% and trading between $100.40 and $104.90. Piper Sandler upgraded the stock to Hold, and reports pointed to a planned 10% price increase on select processors alongside progress on Intel Foundry's advanced lithography rollout.

IBM (IBM) rose roughly 3.2% on the day, trading between $234.76 and $235.96. The move came alongside enterprise-AI and hybrid-cloud momentum and strong cash flow, but the backdrop is mixed: IBM trimmed its full-year constant-currency revenue growth target to 4–5%, HSBC and Oppenheimer both flagged valuation concerns, and law firm Hagens Berman announced an investigation into possible securities-law issues tied to IBM Z mainframe performance disclosures.

Vanguard S&P 500 UCITS ETF (VUAA), as an index tracker, moved in line with the broader U.S. market's gain on the session (see below) — a specific closing print wasn't available from the sources checked.

NVIDIA (NVDA) was little changed, closing around $218.36 versus a $218.36 prior close, ranging between $217.20 and $220.99. The bigger news was corporate rather than price-driven: NVIDIA formally announced its acquisition of Hugging Face, the large-language-model platform, for $12.9 billion.

Moderna (MRNA) was the standout mover, up roughly 8.6% to $147.37, ranging between $135.01 and $149.69. FDA approval of Moderna's updated 2026–2027 COVID-19 vaccines gives it five commercial mRNA products now (COVID-19, combo flu/COVID-19, RSV and influenza); Argus reiterated a Buy with a $180 target, and Morgan Stanley said it sees limited read-across from BioNTech's disappointing colorectal-cancer data to Moderna's own mRNA cancer-vaccine program, which recently posted positive late-stage melanoma results.

ASML (ASML/ASME) had no fresh company-specific news on the session itself; the more recent developments were Morgan Stanley trimming its price target to €1,700 from €1,930 (while keeping an Overweight rating) after ASML's last quarter beat estimates on both earnings and revenue, and a new collaboration with Xanadu Quantum on lithography for photonic quantum hardware.

Broader market: the S&P 500 rose 0.86% to 7,656.98 and the Nasdaq Composite gained 0.96% to 26,333.04, snapping a four-session losing streak. The move followed the August CPI report: headline inflation matched expectations at +0.4%, but core CPI came in hotter than forecast at +0.3% (versus 0.2% consensus), pushing the annual rate to 3.4%. That hotter core print sharply raised the market-implied odds of a Fed rate hike at the next FOMC meeting, with 2-year Treasury yields jumping to a more-than-two-year high.
